# Research findings

Source: a dataset of 15 reels from one engineering explainer channel, posted 22 October to 1 November 2026, plus one 74-second study reel. Findings are patterns only. No scripts, stems or frames from those reels belong in this folder.

## Performance
- Plays ranged from 9,438 to 1,306,537, with a median of 27,874.
- The top reel had 12.5 times the plays of the next one, so it is an outlier. Treat any single-factor explanation as a guess.
- The top three reels had the highest like rates, 3.9 to 5.0%. The rest ranged from 1.4 to 3.3%.
- The top reel had a survival premise and the shortest gap between Buts, 6.3s. With 15 samples, this is only a hypothesis.

## Visual patterns
- The film starts building on frame 0, on a three-quarter angle.
- Cutaway appears when the inside matters, not all the time.
- A small human figure gives scale.
- Mechanism close-ups often go transparent or show a clean section.
- Several reels end on an angle close to the opening. This needs measuring across all 15.

## Corrections made during research
- An early count of 125 events a minute came from separated stems that still held voice residue. Recounting only in voice-silent moments gave a similar rate, and showed most events are small and high, the opposite of the first sound pass.
- Sound events did not line up with caption swaps beyond chance, so there is no per-word tick.
